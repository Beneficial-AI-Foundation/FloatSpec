+`RoundAbsMonotoner` for the closest rounding of `t + v`, and discharge the
+input bound with the triangle inequality. -/

+        Fbounded (beta:=beta) bo b ∧

+        Fcanonic (beta:=beta) radix bo b ∧

+        Fnormal (beta:=beta) radix bo z ∧
